# Vivid Interaction Patterns

Use this reference only to generate or repair Chain Method links. Preserve both target items and make the first act on the second.

## Pattern Families

| Pattern | Mechanism | Compact example |
|---|---|---|
| Collision | Item A crashes into, crushes, or ricochets off item B | A planet-sized apple bowls over a glass tower |
| Transformation | Item A melts, unfolds, or mutates into item B | A key liquefies and hardens into a bicycle |
| Impossible physics | Gravity, time, or material behavior is violated | A feather pulls an anvil upward like a balloon |
| Scale change | One item becomes impossibly large or tiny while interacting | A microscopic bus drives through a needle |
| Body substitution | A non-sensitive body part becomes or operates item B | A hand opens and its fingers turn into pencils |
| Containment/eruption | Item B bursts from inside item A or vice versa | Bread splits and apples erupt across the room |
| Consumption | Item A bites, drinks, or swallows item B | A clock noisily chews through a wooden chair |
| Tool misuse | Item A uses item B in an absurd wrong way | A penguin irons a shirt with a hot pizza |
| Sensory overload | Sound, texture, smell, color, or temperature becomes extreme | Milk screams like a siren while freezing a loaf |
| Multiplication | Item A produces an impossible number of item B | One coin sneezes out ten thousand umbrellas |

## Quality Check

A usable link answers yes to all four:

1. Are both target items recognizable?
2. Does item A visibly affect item B?
3. Is the action directional enough to cue A then B?
4. Is at least one feature exaggerated, sensory, or impossible?

Do not default to graphic violence or sexual imagery. If the user explicitly requests adult/disturbing imagery and it is appropriate, keep it non-graphic and within safety constraints. A comic impossibility is usually more portable and equally distinctive.
