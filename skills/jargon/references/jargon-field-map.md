# Jargon Seed Priming Reference Map

Use this reference to prime foundational vocabularies, analogies, and explanations across high-friction technical domains.

---

## 1. Machine Learning & Artificial Intelligence

| Term | Analogy / Metaphor | Plain English Explanation |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Transformer** | A group conversation moderator who knows exactly who is speaking to whom and who to pay attention to at any given moment. | A neural network design that processes data (like words in a sentence) by tracking relationships and attention weights between all words simultaneously, rather than one by one. |
| **Overfitting** | Memorizing the specific questions and answers of a practice exam instead of understanding the underlying concepts, causing you to fail the real test. | A scenario where an AI model learns the training data's noise and details too well, making it perform poorly on new, unseen data. |
| **Gradient Descent** | Walking down a dark hill in a heavy fog by feeling the slope of the ground under your feet to find the absolute lowest point (the valley). | An optimization method used to train AI models by taking small steps in the direction that reduces calculation errors (slopes) the fastest. |

## 2. Distributed Systems & Blockchains

| Term | Analogy / Metaphor | Plain English Explanation |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Consensus Mechanism** | A boardroom of business partners who must all sign a single ledger book before any transaction is considered official. | A protocol or rule system used by multiple connected computers to agree on a single data value or ledger state across an untrusted network. |
| **Reentrancy** | A bank customer who manages to withdraw money from their account repeatedly before the teller has a chance to update their balance sheet. | A security flaw where a computer program (often a smart contract) is interrupted mid-execution to run another process, allowing it to perform unauthorized actions before completing the initial task. |
| **Sharding** | Splitting a massive encyclopedia set across ten different bookshelves so that ten people can look up different volumes at the same time. | A database partitioning technique that breaks up data into smaller, independent pieces (shards) distributed across multiple servers to improve speed. |

---

## 3. Quantitative Finance & Corporate Valuations

| Term | Analogy / Metaphor | Plain English Explanation |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Arbitrage** | Buying a concert ticket for $50 outside the stadium and immediately walking inside to sell it to someone else for $80. | The practice of taking advantage of price differences in two or more markets, securing a risk-free profit by buying low and selling high simultaneously. |
| **Liquidity** | The ease of selling a bicycle (very fast for cash) compared to selling a house (takes months of legal paperwork). | A measure of how quickly and easily an asset can be converted into cash without affecting its market price. |
| **Hedging** | Buying travel insurance for a non-refundable trip in case you get sick and cannot go. | An investment strategy designed to reduce risk by taking an offset position in a related asset to limit potential losses. |
